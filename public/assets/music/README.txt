Soundtrack files (supplied by the project owner under the team's licence).
The game streams them by scene and cross-fades between them; a missing file
falls back to the original music generated in the browser.

  menu.mp3      title, menus, character / chapter select   ("Fight")
  dialogue.mp3  story dialogue scenes and the Dojo          (Toshiro Masuda)
  battle.mp3    the whole fight, loops                      (Madara, Perfect Susanoo theme)
  victory.mp3   victory screen, plays once                  ("Blue Bird")
  boss.mp3      optional: not used (one track per fight)
